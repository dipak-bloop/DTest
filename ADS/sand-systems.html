SAND PULSE - AUTOMATED SAND DUMP SYSTEM

Standalone ABP systems provide precise back pressure control for Managed Pressure Drilling operations. Integrated measurement tools such as Multiphase meters support accurate flow monitoring and improved pressure management during drilling operations.
SYSTEM CONFIGURATION
  • Sand Dump Manifold
  • Hydraulic Isolation valves
  • Well Test Chokes
Key Advantages
  • Patented Sand Dump System
  • Remove personnel from red zone, remote monitoring and operation
  • Rapid deployment in under 3 hours with an intuitive touchscreen interface and minimal operator training required
  • Automatically dumps sand from SKO’s driven by various data triggers that can be selected
  • Allows full control of multiple SKOS on location with interlocked controls for safety
  • Up to 8 well test chokes controlled from single control system, multiple inputs for control sequencing
TECHNICAL SNAPSHOT
  • Options to control 4 & 8 SKO's per system
  • Hazardous area rated - 15,000 psi system design
  • Mulitple dump modes: Manual, Interval, Schedule, P & Volume-based (see data sheet point 4 for symbol infront of 'P')
  • 1-second hi-fi data logging & SCADA integration - pressures, flows & sand events
